Value model

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × category × chains × readiness

growth = 1 + clamp(7-day change, −50%, +150%) × 0.6  ·  maturity = 0.65 + 0.55 × exp(−((age − 730) / 1000)²)  ·  chains = 1 + 0.02 × extra chains (max 5)  ·  readiness = 0.15 for already-traded products, 1.00 otherwise. Age unknown counts as 365 days.
